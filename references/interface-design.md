# Desktop interface design

## Information architecture

Use three columns: songs and orientation on the left; song, progress, prompts, future groups, two-row keyboard, 61-key reference, statistics, and demo controls in the center; settings, metronome, and playing guidance on the right.

Brand styling may vary. Preserve the information hierarchy and the dominant center work area.

## Stable practice area

Reserve a fixed or sufficient minimum height for the current prompt. Single notes, chords, rests, completion, and waiting must keep the next section at the same vertical position. Verify bounding boxes across states.

Future groups use letters. The keyboard shows Q–P/A–L, targets, held state, and hand zone. Do not rely on color alone.

## Pitch reference and scope

Show C2–C7. Original pitches outside the view still sound unchanged. An octave-adapted option must identify conversions and leave source data untouched.

Use semantic controls, visible focus, sufficient contrast, status text, and reduced-motion support. Initial scope is desktop only.
